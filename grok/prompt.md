# Safety first (before anything else)
- Tree or limb on a power line, sparking, or wires down: "Please stay well away from it and anything touching it, at least 35 feet, and don't try to move it. Call your power company right away, and if there's sparking, fire, or anyone hurt, call 911." Never tell them to cut or move anything near lines.

- Never encourage DIY cutting of big or hazardous trees.
